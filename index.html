What Global adds

Global also links to records in trial registries outside the US and EU, such as China's ChiCTR and other WHO-listed national registries, and flags papers from studies run in other jurisdictions.

Why it's labelled

These studies may be run under regulatory, ethics and data standards that are different from, and sometimes lighter than, those of the US FDA or the EU's EMA. Registry records can be less complete or less often updated. Each result is tagged "non-FDA/EMA registry" with its source and country.

Not advice

Showing a study is not an endorsement or a recommendation of any study, drug, sponsor or country, and is not medical, legal or investment advice.

    What it tracks

    ClinicalTrials.gov

    New and updated studies, phases, status changes and completion dates.

    openFDA

    Drug approvals, submissions and label changes.

    NIH RePORTER

    Funded grants by topic, institution and investigator.

    PubMed

    Peer-reviewed literature as soon as it's indexed.

    Europe PMC

    Full-text open access papers and citations.

    bioRxiv

    Preprints before they hit journals.

    SEC EDGAR

    PDUFA date candidates pulled from company filings.

    EMA (EU)

    European Medicines Agency opinions, approvals, refusals and withdrawals. Source: EMA.

    FDA advisory committees

    Upcoming AdCom meetings, the drugs on the agenda, postponements and cancellations. Source: Federal Register.

    FDA complete response letters

    Why FDA declined an NDA or BLA: facility, manufacturing, clinical or safety issues, with a link to the letter. Source: FDA / openFDA.

    FDA decision calendar

    Upcoming PDUFA dates disclosed in company 8-Ks, AdCom meetings, approvals and CRLs in one list, filterable by drug, company or ticker and condition. Watches flag new events and moved dates. Each event shows its source and confidence. Sources: SEC EDGAR, Federal Register, FDA / openFDA.

    Trial readout calendar

    Upcoming primary completion dates across the market, filterable by sponsor or ticker, condition, phase and date window. Watches flag slipped dates. Browse the calendar. Source: ClinicalTrials.gov.

    Retraction warning

    Check up to 50 papers (PMIDs or DOIs) for retractions, expressions of concern and corrections, with links to each notice and how many sources agree. Free on the hosted MCP endpoint (tool paper_retraction_check). Informational only, not medical or investment advice. Sources: PubMed, Crossref (including Retraction Watch data), OpenAlex.

    Trials at Risk

    Trials that were suspended, terminated or withdrawn, are past their primary completion date, slipped, or fell short of target enrollment. Each trial is scored by risk, with Markdown, HTML, RSS and JSON digests. Source: ClinicalTrials.gov.

    Quickstart

    CLI

    pip install biocatalystfeed
bcf --help
bcf trials "KRAS G12C"
bcf papers "GLP-1 obesity"

    Run bcf --help for every command and source.

    MCP server

    pip install biocatalystfeed
biocatalystfeed-mcp

    Speaks the Model Context Protocol, so any MCP client can call it.

    Use inside Claude or Cursor

    Add the server to your MCP config (claude_desktop_config.json for Claude Desktop, .cursor/mcp.json for Cursor):

    {
  "mcpServers": {
    "biocatalystfeed": { "command": "biocatalystfeed-mcp" }
  }
}

    Then ask: "What phase 3 oncology trials changed status this week?"
